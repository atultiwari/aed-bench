For clinicians

More

The big idea: The AED decides; you keep the blood moving. Push hard and fast until it's on, hands off only while it reads and shocks, then push again at once.

A teaching figure, not a substitute for a CPR course. In a real emergency, call your emergency number and follow the AED's voice. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number (911 in the US and Canada).

  • Shock energy: Set by the AED (usually 150-200 J biphasic for adults). Rate 110/min (100–120), depth 5–6 cm, 30:2 with a 5 s pause for two breaths. Long CPR stations are shown 3 s at real speed, then fast-forwarded (×15); the speed chip always says how fast. Compression fraction = time pushing ÷ time since the first push (breath pauses count as no pushing); hands-off = the AED's pauses only.
